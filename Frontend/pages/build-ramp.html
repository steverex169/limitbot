Build a ramp

Follow Pinnacle live at a share you choose. The ramp is not configured — it happens, because Pinnacle raises a limit as a game takes money.

A game takes almost no money in the morning and most of it near the start, and Pinnacle's limit climbs with it. Each limit below is checked against Pinnacle every few minutes and moved to your share of whatever it is at that moment, so the ramp follows the market rather than a clock.
